# Medical disclaimer

ZuriTrustAI provides general maternal-health information and preliminary danger-sign screening to help people decide **how urgently** to seek care and **where**. It:

- does **not** diagnose any condition or recommend treatment or medicine doses;
- does **not** replace antenatal, postnatal or emergency care from a qualified health worker;
- has **not** been clinically validated. Results reported in `eval/` are software tests on fictional scenarios.

Clinical content is a draft pending review and sign-off by the Zuri Circle Chief Medical Officer. Facility data in `facilities.sample.json` is fictional and for development only.

**In an emergency, go to the nearest health facility or call the local emergency number immediately.**
